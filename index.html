01 · Problem

Analysts had the data—but not a fast, trustworthy path to an explanation.

20–160 min

Internal estimate for recurring analysis preparation, depending on the required data and log research.

Find the right source

Different questions depended on different snapshots, sessions, and datasets.

Reconstruct the comparison

Users downloaded large files and repeatedly rebuilt spreadsheet pivots.

Investigate the cause

High-level deltas did not reveal the records, drivers, or ownership underneath.

Defend the answer

AI-generated text alone was insufficient without visible evidence and traceability.

02 · Embedded research

I treated delivery conversations and live usage as a continuous research loop.

This was embedded product research rather than a standalone formal study. I used contextual inquiry, co-design, demos, pilots, production feedback, and support signals while engineering the product.

Research boundary: these findings came from repeated collaboration with planning partners and users—not a statistically representative or controlled usability study.
Co-design

Reviewed workflow assumptions, integration options, controls, hierarchy, and drill-down behavior with a domain engineer.

Planning feedback

Collected recurring needs and specific usability issues from a senior planning SME and active users.

Demo observation

Used early walkthroughs to identify hesitation, lost context, unclear system state, and evidence needs.

Production signals

Used enhancement requests, support questions, failures, and later usage reporting as behavioral evidence.

03 · Task-flow redesign

Center the workflow on the question—not files and backend systems.

Before

  1. Interpret the stakeholder question
  2. Find datasets and snapshots
  3. Download large files
  4. Build pivots manually
  5. Search logs and records
  6. Assemble the explanation

After

  1. Choose analysis intent
  2. Select baseline and comparison
  3. Inspect the visual change
  4. Narrow scope with filters
  5. Read the synchronized explanation
  6. Trace records and drivers

05 · Evaluation and iteration

Feedback became specific, shipped design changes.

Planning-SME feedbackUsers trusted charts before generated text

Moved to visual-first hierarchy and configurable chart types.

Pilot and production feedbackFilters disappeared between views

Preserved state across charts, pivots, and summaries.

User enhancement requestNarrative did not reflect narrowed scope

Sent visible filters with insight requests and regeneration.

Demo observationThe page felt slow while waiting

Separated data, visual, and insight loading paths.

Early-use feedbackUsers could trigger duplicate requests

Added disabled Analyze state and clearer progress behavior.

Co-design and bug reportsDense dropdowns slowed selection

Improved filtering, defaults, placeholders, and width.
