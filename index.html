about

I’m a software engineer who kept gravitating toward the space between a messy workflow and a usable product.

I like talking to the people doing the work, finding the point where a process stops making sense, and making the interaction concrete enough to test in code. Then I stay with it through implementation, UAT, production feedback, and the less glamorous fixes that make a product trustworthy.

Most of my recent work has been in complex internal systems where visual polish matters, but accurate states, understandable rules, and safe actions matter more.

how I work

My process is not a handoff from design to engineering. I use each activity to make the next decision more concrete.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the real workflow.

I map the questions, handoffs, workarounds, ownership boundaries, and hidden rules people rely on today.

02 / MAKE

Use prototypes to clarify decisions.

I move between task flows, interface structure, and coded interactions so feedback is grounded in something testable.

03 / LEARN

Treat delivery as research.

UAT, production discrepancies, support questions, and debugging sessions reveal where the system model and user model differ.

04 / BUILD

Keep the implementation honest.

I make loading, empty, error, eligibility, and completed states explicit instead of smoothing over uncertainty in the interface.

what I care about

I’m drawn to products that have to explain themselves: systems with dense information, consequential actions, and users who know immediately when the interface does not match reality.
